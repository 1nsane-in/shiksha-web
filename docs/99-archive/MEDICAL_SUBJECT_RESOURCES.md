# Medical Subject Resources

Last checked: 2026-11-18

Vetted resources for physiology, biochemistry, pharmacology, pathology, and microbiology. The goal is not "free to view"; it is legally safe to embed in a commercial platform.

Companion docs: [Histology Resources](HISTOLOGY_RESOURCES.md), [Topographic Anatomy Resources](TOPOGRAPHIC_ANATOMY_RESOURCES.md).

**Rule:** "free to view" does not mean "free to reuse." Use CC BY, CC BY-SA, CC0, public domain, or BSD-style assets. Avoid CC BY-NC, CC BY-ND, and unlicensed copyrighted material unless you have written permission.

---

## Quick picks

| Subject | Best commercial-safe base | Main gap |
|---|---|---|
| Physiology | Fundamentals of Anatomy and Physiology (Australian Edition), CC BY-SA; Introductory Animal Physiology, CC BY | Human physiology depth. Adapt or write additional text. |
| Biochemistry | UniProt, RCSB PDB, AlphaFold DB (CC BY/CC0 data) | No clean CC BY textbook found. Write the text and use open structure data. |
| Pharmacology | Open RN Nursing Pharmacology 2e, CC BY; IUPHAR/BPS Guide to Pharmacology, ODbL + CC BY-SA | Drug data changes often. Keep content versioned. |
| Pathology | Human Protein Atlas, PLISM, CAMELYON16, CDC PHIL (CC BY/CC0/public domain) | No clean CC BY textbook found. Write the text; use open images. |
| Microbiology | Microbiology, Pharmacology, and Immunology for Pre-Clinical Students, CC BY; Microbiology for Allied Health Students, CC BY | Check chapter-level third-party exceptions. |

## Cross-subject traps

1. **OpenStax 2e is not CC BY.** Anatomy & Physiology 2e, Microbiology 2e, Biology 2e, and Chemistry 2e are CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. Older 1e prefaces still say CC BY 4.0, but live site terms control. Treat as NC unless you verify an archived CC BY copy.
2. **"Adapted from OpenStax" is not a license.** Some remixes are CC BY because the adapter had permission or used an older edition; others inherit NC-SA. Check the actual book page.
3. **CC BY-SA is commercial-safe, with conditions.** Unmodified use with attribution is usually fine. Adapted content must be shared under the same license. Get legal review before building a proprietary derivative around it.
4. **US government works are public domain, but embedded third-party images may not be.** Check captions and credits.
5. **Licenses change.** Keep a local snapshot of the license text and retrieval date for every asset you ship.

## Recommended implementation for Shiksha

1. **Use commercial-safe bases first.** Start with the quick-pick resources; do not embed NC textbooks or atlases in a paid product.
2. **Write the missing text.** Biochemistry and pathology have open data/images but no clean CC BY textbook. Build concise in-house text around open figures.
3. **Keep citations visible.** Add a credits/sources page and store source URL, author, license, and retrieval date.
4. **Separate content from assets.** Keep textbook text, images, and generated figures in separate stores so a license change affects only one layer.
5. **Link out to restricted resources.** Pathology Outlines, WebPathology, DrugBank, and similar sites are references, not source assets.
6. **Version medical content.** Drug approvals, labels, and guidelines change. Record review dates and re-check before exams or admissions cycles.
7. **Legal review for share-alike and 3D.** CC BY-SA adaptations, 3D meshes, and derived renders need a license review before shipping.

## License checklist

- [ ] Confirm the exact license for every image, table, and chapter, not just the parent site.
- [ ] Record source URL, author, license, and retrieval date in a credits file.
- [ ] Reject CC BY-NC, CC BY-ND, and unlicensed copyrighted assets for commercial use.
- [ ] Check third-party exceptions in aggregate textbooks and databases.
- [ ] Keep a local copy of the license text used at retrieval time.
- [ ] For CC BY-SA adaptations, confirm share-alike obligations with legal.
- [ ] For datasets, confirm the license covers derived figures and renders.
- [ ] Re-check OpenStax and other OER licenses before each content release.
