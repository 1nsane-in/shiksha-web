# Privacy Policy



## 1. PRIVACY POLICY

### 1.1 Identity and scope

This Privacy Policy is issued by Shiksha International Private Limited (“Shiksha”, “Company”, “we”, “us” or “our”), CIN U70200RJ2026PTC110510, having its registered office at 122, Nider, Manglam Vihar, Jaipur, Rajasthan, 302013, India. It applies to https://www.shiksha.study, Shiksha Academy, student and partner portals, enquiry forms, admission services, events, communications and other services that link to this Policy.

This Policy explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, how we use and disclose it, how long we retain it, how we protect it and how individuals may contact us. Where a separate notice or consent is presented for a specific activity, that notice supplements this Policy.

### 1.2 Categories of personal data

- Identity and contact data: name, date of birth, contact details, address, nationality and country of residence.
- Education and application data: school/university records, marksheets, certificates, entrance-test results, preferred courses, application status and correspondence.
- Identity and travel documents: passport, photograph, visa documents and travel information where required for the requested service.
- Financial and transaction data: payment amount, currency, transaction reference, payer/payee details, invoice, refund and reconciliation records. Full card credentials, PINs and banking passwords should be handled by payment providers and not collected by Shiksha.
- Account and usage data: login identifiers, authentication events, device/browser details, IP address, session data, preferences, learning progress, quiz attempts, subscription status and support requests.
- Partner and representative data: business contact details, authorisation documents, commission records and contractual information.
- Special or sensitive information: where voluntarily supplied or necessary, limited health, disability or other sensitive information relevant to an application or accommodation request. We will seek specific notice/consent and restrict access where required.

### 1.3 Sources of data

We collect information directly from the individual, a parent/guardian or authorised representative, through the website and portals, from universities or service partners in connection with an application, from payment providers, and from publicly available sources where lawful and relevant. If a third party submits information about another person, that third party must have lawful authority to do so.

### 1.4 Purposes and lawful basis

We use personal data only for stated, legitimate and proportionate purposes, including responding to enquiries; assessing and processing admissions requests; preparing and transmitting applications; communicating with universities; providing requested student support; managing accounts; providing Academy access; delivering lectures and learning analytics; processing payments and refunds; preventing fraud; maintaining security; resolving complaints; complying with law; and sending marketing communications where permitted.

Where consent is required, we will request it through a clear affirmative action and record the relevant notice/version and consent event. Where processing is necessary to provide a requested service, perform a contract, comply with law or pursue another legally permitted purpose, we will rely only on the applicable legal basis. Marketing consent will be separate from essential service communications and may be withdrawn.

### 1.5 Admission data and disclosures to universities

For an application, Shiksha may disclose relevant documents and details to the university selected or authorised by the applicant, and to a visa, travel, accommodation or other provider only where necessary for the requested service or otherwise lawfully permitted. Before transmitting application documents, Shiksha will identify the intended recipient or category of recipient and purpose, and obtain any consent or authority required by law. Applicants should review documents for accuracy before submission.

### 1.6 International transfers

Because Shiksha supports overseas education, data may be accessed or transferred to universities, authorised representatives, cloud providers and service providers in countries outside India, including the country of the selected university. We will make such transfers only where permitted by applicable Indian law and any applicable law in the recipient jurisdiction, and will use contractual, technical and organisational safeguards appropriate to the risk. The recipient country and recipient categories should be disclosed in the relevant application notice where practicable.

### 1.7 Service providers and processors

We may use vetted providers for hosting, cloud storage, email/SMS, customer support, video delivery, learning management, analytics, payment processing, identity verification and cybersecurity. Contracts should require confidentiality, purpose limitation, security controls, breach notification, restricted subcontracting, deletion/return of data and audit or compliance evidence appropriate to the service. A provider may process data only on documented instructions unless it independently determines its purposes under applicable law.

### 1.8 Payments and tuition-fee information

Where a student pays a university directly, Shiksha may receive a receipt or transaction confirmation to verify application or fee status. Where Shiksha is involved in collecting or facilitating a university payment, the payment notice and receipt must identify the actual payee, the intended university beneficiary, the amount and currency, the service/transaction reference, any disclosed charges, the remittance channel and the party responsible for issuing the university receipt. Payment processing must follow the bank-approved and legally reviewed structure. Shiksha will not request a user’s banking password, card PIN or one-time password.

### 1.9 Cookies and analytics

The website may use strictly necessary cookies for login, security and core functionality, and optional analytics or advertising technologies where permitted. A cookie banner or preference centre should allow users to accept, reject or configure non-essential cookies. The website should link to a separate cookie list describing provider, purpose, duration and category. Browser controls may also be used, although disabling necessary cookies can affect functionality.

### 1.10 Security safeguards

Shiksha will maintain a documented security programme proportionate to the nature and volume of data, including role-based access, least privilege, MFA for privileged and sensitive systems, encryption in transit and at rest where appropriate, secure backups, vulnerability and patch management, logging and monitoring, endpoint protection, staff confidentiality obligations, vendor due diligence and periodic access reviews. Passport, financial and sensitive records must be stored in restricted repositories and must not be circulated through personal email or informal messaging groups.

No internet transmission or storage system is completely secure. Users should use unique passwords, protect their devices and notify Shiksha promptly of suspected account compromise.

### 1.11 Retention and deletion

Shiksha retains data only for as long as reasonably necessary for the stated purpose, contractual administration, university and financial reconciliation, statutory recordkeeping, dispute resolution, security and legitimate legal claims. The Company must maintain an internal retention schedule specifying data category, retention trigger, period, legal basis and deletion method. When retention is no longer required, data will be securely deleted or anonymised, subject to legal holds and mandatory recordkeeping.

### 1.12 Children and parental/guardian involvement

Some applicants may be under 18. Where applicable law requires verifiable parental or guardian consent, Shiksha will obtain it before processing the child’s personal data for the relevant purpose. Shiksha will not knowingly undertake behavioural monitoring or targeted advertising to children where prohibited. The parent/guardian’s authority may be verified proportionately. Age-related requirements must be implemented in the registration and application workflows.

The response team will contain the incident, preserve evidence, assess impact, remediate, document decisions and notify affected persons and authorities within the time and manner required by applicable law. A public-facing policy does not replace operational breach procedures.
