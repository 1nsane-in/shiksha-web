# Refund, Cancellation and Payment Policy


## 2. REFUND, CANCELLATION AND PAYMENT POLICY

### 2.1 Scope and governing principle

This Policy applies to payments made to Shiksha International Private Limited or facilitated by Shiksha for admission consultancy, application coordination, university tuition and institutional charges, visa/travel support, Shiksha Academy subscriptions and other services. The applicable quotation, invoice, university fee schedule, signed service agreement and payment instructions must be read together with this Policy. Mandatory rights under consumer and other applicable laws are not excluded.

### 2.2 Two distinct university payment routes

**ROUTE A — DIRECT PAYMENT TO UNIVERSITY:** The student pays tuition or institutional charges directly to the university’s official bank account or authorised payment channel. Shiksha may separately invoice its own consultancy or service fee. The university controls its own tuition-fee refund decision and timetable. Shiksha will provide reasonable documentation assistance where included in the service, but does not control the university’s funds.

**ROUTE B — PAYMENT FACILITATED THROUGH SHIKSHA:** This route may be used only where the precise payment flow has been reviewed and approved in writing by Shiksha’s Authorised Dealer bank and qualified FEMA/RBI counsel, and is permitted under all applicable laws. Before payment, the student must receive a transaction-specific payment notice identifying the legal recipient of funds, the university beneficiary, the purpose, amount, currency, exchange-rate basis, charges, expected processing timeline, refund route and responsible parties. The Company must not use this clause as authority to receive or hold funds in a manner prohibited by FEMA, RBI directions or its bank. If the approved structure requires payment directly from the student’s bank account to the university beneficiary, Shiksha will use that structure rather than routing tuition funds through its ordinary operating account.

For Route B, Shiksha will issue a receipt only for the amount it actually receives or processes, clearly distinguishing (i) tuition/institutional money, (ii) Shiksha service fees, (iii) bank/payment charges and (iv) any other third-party charge. The university’s official receipt or confirmation should be provided to the student when received. Shiksha will maintain transaction-level reconciliation and evidence of remittance.

### 2.3 Separate fee categories

Every quotation or invoice should clearly distinguish: (a) university tuition and institutional fees; (b) Shiksha consultancy/application/service fees; (c) government, embassy, visa and examination charges; (d) accommodation, insurance, travel and other third-party charges; (e) payment gateway, bank and foreign-exchange charges; and (f) taxes. No amount should be described as a university fee unless it is imposed by or payable for the university.

### 2.4 Shiksha consultancy and service fees

Before accepting payment, Shiksha will disclose the scope of work, fee, milestones, cancellation terms and any third-party costs. If the student cancels before work begins, Shiksha will assess a refund of the unearned service fee, less only costs that were clearly disclosed, actually incurred and lawfully deductible. If work has begun, Shiksha may retain a reasonable, proportionate amount for services actually performed and documented, plus properly disclosed non-recoverable third-party costs. Any balance will be refunded where required by law or the contract.

Shiksha will not apply an arbitrary or punitive cancellation deduction, nor treat the entire service fee as automatically earned merely because an application has been opened. A milestone schedule should state the work included at each stage, such as counselling, document review, application preparation, submission and post-offer coordination.

### 2.5 University tuition and institutional fees

University tuition, registration, hostel, examination, insurance or other institutional charges are subject to the university’s written refund rules and the applicable enrolment agreement. Shiksha cannot promise a university refund unless the university has confirmed the entitlement. Where the student paid the university directly, the student ordinarily submits the refund request to the university, with reasonable assistance from Shiksha if included in the service. Where a payment was lawfully facilitated by Shiksha, Shiksha will promptly submit or support the request and pass on any amount actually received or returned for the student, subject to lawful deductions and the transaction-specific payment terms.

If the university refunds money directly to the student, Shiksha will not collect that same amount again. If the university refunds money to Shiksha or the authorised payment channel, the amount attributable to the student will be reconciled and returned through the permitted channel. Shiksha will provide a calculation and supporting transaction reference on request.

### 2.6 Admission rejection, visa refusal and student withdrawal

An admission rejection or visa refusal does not automatically create a right to a full refund from Shiksha. The refund outcome depends on the reason, the stage of service, the university’s written rules, government/third-party charges and the applicable service agreement. Shiksha service fees for work already properly performed may remain payable, subject to law. Where rejection results from Shiksha’s proven material error or breach, the matter will be reviewed and an appropriate remedy provided under the contract and applicable law.

If a student withdraws, defers, changes university or course, fails to provide documents, misses a deadline or fails to meet eligibility requirements, the student must notify Shiksha and the university promptly. University charges and Shiksha fees will be assessed separately.

### 2.7 Shiksha Academy and digital subscriptions

The product page must state the subscription term, price, included subjects and features, access start date, renewal terms, device/user limits and any trial. Access may begin immediately after successful payment or at the stated activation date. Users may cancel future renewal at any time through [account settings/contact method]. Cancellation stops future renewal but does not by itself refund an already elapsed or commenced subscription period, except where a refund is required by law, the service was materially misdescribed, duplicate/unauthorised payment is confirmed, or Shiksha cannot provide the purchased service and cannot reasonably remedy the failure.

If a digital product has not been activated or access has not been made available, a refund request will be assessed with reference to the purchase description, applicable law and actual circumstances. Shiksha will not use a blanket “no refunds under any circumstances” clause. Any cooling-off, withdrawal or digital-content consent requirement applicable to the transaction must be implemented in the checkout flow.

### 2.8 Duplicate, mistaken or unauthorised payments

Customers should report duplicate, excess, wrong-beneficiary or suspected unauthorised transactions promptly to [refund email]. Shiksha will investigate using the payment reference and provider records. Confirmed duplicate or excess amounts will be refunded or reversed through the original or legally permitted channel. Suspected fraud may require identity verification and coordination with the bank/payment provider.

### 2.9 Currency, exchange rates and bank charges

For cross-border transactions, the payment notice must specify the currency in which the student is charged, the currency expected by the university, the exchange-rate source/time or bank quote, any conversion spread or service charge, and who bears intermediary or receiving-bank charges. Exchange-rate movements and intermediary deductions outside Shiksha’s control may affect the amount credited. Shiksha must not conceal or retrospectively invent a foreign-exchange charge.

### 2.10 Refund request procedure and timelines

Requests should be sent to [refund email] with the student’s name, registered email/mobile, invoice, transaction reference, payment date, service/university, reason and supporting documents. Shiksha will acknowledge complaints within the applicable legal period and communicate the decision, calculation and any documents still required. Approved refunds will be initiated within [10–15] working days where Shiksha controls the payment, unless a shorter statutory period applies or the funds are awaiting a university, bank or payment-provider reversal. For third-party-controlled funds, Shiksha will provide status updates and evidence of follow-up. The actual credit date depends on the bank/payment channel.

### 2.11 Refund exclusions and non-excludable rights

Government, embassy, university and third-party fees may be non-refundable under the rules of the relevant authority or provider. Shiksha will identify such charges before payment where reasonably possible. No exclusion applies where a refund or other remedy is mandatory under applicable law, where Shiksha has charged for a service not supplied, or where the charge is otherwise unlawful.

### 2.12 Chargebacks and disputes

Customers should contact Shiksha promptly to resolve payment issues. This does not restrict the customer’s right to contact their bank, payment provider or consumer authority. Shiksha may submit transaction records, consent records, invoices and service-delivery evidence to respond to a chargeback or dispute, subject to law.
